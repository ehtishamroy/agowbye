Before you buy

Questions parents ask us.

Still unsure? Email hello@agow.ca and a real person replies within one business day.

Can I use it from birth?+

Yes. The seat reclines nearly flat for newborns, or add the bassinet (included in the Newborn Set) for the first six months. It then works as a toddler seat up to 22 kg, around 4 years.

Does it work with my car seat?+

Included adapters fit most infant car seats sold in Canada, including Maxi-Cosi, Cybex, Nuna and Clek. Not sure about yours? Send us the model and we'll confirm.

Is it good in snow?+

The wide rubber wheels and four-wheel suspension handle packed snow and salted sidewalks. For deep snow, lock the front wheels straight with one click.

How fast is shipping?+

Free, tracked shipping anywhere in Canada. Most orders arrive in 3–6 business days. You'll get tracking by email the day it ships.

What if it's not right for us?+

You have 100 nights. Email us, we arrange a free pickup and refund you in full within 5 business days of receiving it.

Can I pay in instalments?+

Yes. Choose Shop Pay at checkout to split the price into 4 interest-free payments.
